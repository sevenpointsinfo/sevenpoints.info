About Seven Points

Excellence in Every Detail

With 150+ completed projects and 100% on-time delivery, we've established ourselves as the premier choice for premium construction and event management.

Seven Points is a premier construction and event management company based in Duhok, Kurdistan Region. With over 5 years of proven excellence, we have successfully delivered 150+ projects that define luxury, innovation, and precision.

Our expertise spans construction management, interior design, event production, and comprehensive site supervision. We work exclusively with high-end clients who demand excellence, reliability, and attention to detail in every project phase.

From large-scale construction projects to sophisticated event productions, our dedicated team combines strategic planning, rigorous execution, and client-focused service. We don't just build spaces—we create experiences that reflect our clients' vision and values.
